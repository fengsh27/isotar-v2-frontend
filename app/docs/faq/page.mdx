# FAQ & troubleshooting

## "lncRNA ID(s) cannot be used"

The lncRNA column of a ceRNA pair takes **Ensembl transcript IDs** (`ENST…`). This message appears
when you enter:

- a lncRNA **symbol**, such as `MAGI2-AS3`, `PELATON` or `LINC01614`; or
- a lncRNA **gene** ID, such as `ENSG00000234456`. Predictions are made per transcript, so a gene ID
  matches nothing.

Look the lncRNA up on [Ensembl](https://www.ensembl.org) and use one of its transcript IDs. See
[Which transcript should I use?](/docs/mir-network#3-cerna-pairs)

## "gene(s) are not in the reference for this species"

The gene symbol or RefSeq ID was not found in the 3′ UTR reference of the species and genome you
chose. Check the spelling, use the official symbol rather than an alias, and make sure the species
is right (`TP53` is human, `Trp53` is mouse).

## My network result is empty, or some of my pairs are missing

A pair appears only when the miRNA is predicted to bind **both** the gene and the lncRNA. A missing
pair is a prediction result, not an error. Things to try:

- **Try the lncRNA's other transcripts.** A miRNA often binds only some of a lncRNA's transcripts.
  Add each transcript as its own line with the same gene.
- **Check that the pair belongs to this miRNA.** A pair from a study of a different miRNA tests a
  different hypothesis.
- **Check each side alone.** Run [miR-Target](/run?workflow=mir-target) with the gene, or
  [miR-LncRNA](/run?workflow=mir-lncrna) with the lncRNA, to see which side is not predicted.

## Should I use hg19 or hg38?

Use **hg38** unless your data are aligned to hg19. The two references have different transcript
sets, so a transcript ID from one may be missing from the other, and the hg38 lncRNA reference is
much larger (about 36,000 lncRNA genes, against about 14,000 for hg19). Use the same genome for every
job you want to compare.

## Why can't I choose TargetScan?

TargetScan scores against its own precomputed 3′ UTR alignments, so it is available only for
human, mouse, fruit fly, zebrafish and *C. elegans*, and never for lncRNAs.

## Why does a network job take only one miRNA?

The correlations you enter, corr(miRNA, gene) and corr(miRNA, lncRNA), refer to one miRNA. To compare
forms of that miRNA, add **variants** in the same job. To test another miRNA, start another job.

## My correlations are negative. Can I use them?

Yes. Correlations are read as magnitudes, so −0.6 counts as 0.6. The **What will be submitted**
table shows the conversion. Values above 1 are rejected.

## I can't find my jobs on another computer

The **Jobs** page lists the jobs submitted from the current browser only. Keep each job page's link,
which ends in the job ID; it opens the result from any computer.

## A job failed or looks wrong

Note the **job ID** at the top of the job page and send it to us with a short description. The job
ID lets us trace exactly what ran.
